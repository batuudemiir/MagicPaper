## Sahne promptu

Use case: illustration-story. Create a landscape 4:3 children's storybook page illustration for MagicPaper, no typography, no UI, no frame. Use attached approved reference for matte gouache pigment, soft painted paper forms, expressive restrained modern children's publishing aesthetic. Show an intimate concrete moment: the SAME little girl from the LEFT panel of the reference, same warm skin, shoulder-length wavy dark brown hair, coral long sleeve sweater, cropped dark purple trousers and charcoal ankle boots, now crouching in a quiet garden noticing ONE tiny turquoise sprout with two leaves. Preserve her facial design, age and proportions; this is a fictional demo character, not a new personalized identity. No backpack needed in garden, no new accessories. She looks attentively down at sprout with small curious smile, one hand near but not touching it. A small simple coral watering can rests nearby. Asymmetric editorial composition: girl on right half, sprout lower middle-left, only 2-3 cropped lilac/turquoise garden plant shapes at far edges, light lilac background opening to WHITE at lower edge. Purple #6A47B8, pale lilac #F6F3FC, turquoise, restrained coral. Clearly visible imperfect brush marks, matte pigment, uncomplicated forms. Keep sprout and face large enough at 350px wide. No Ragi, no fox, no castle, no moon, no winding path, no sparkles, no cinematic glow, no 3D gloss, no ornamental wreath, no cream/beige main background, no words. Art stops naturally into clean white at the bottom to meet separate reading text.

## Küçük anlatı resmi promptu

Use case: illustration-story. One SMALL standalone editorial spot illustration for a MagicPaper children's storybook page: a simple coral painted watering can tipped slightly toward a tiny turquoise two-leaf sprout rooted in a small irregular plum soil patch, exactly TWO tiny turquoise water drops falling from spout toward soil. No humans, no mascot, no faces on objects, no eyes, no text, no frame, no scenery, no extra leaves. Match the attached approved art reference's matte gouache painted paper texture, imperfect dry-brush edges and soft restrained contemporary children's book shapes. Palette coral watering can, turquoise sprout/drops, lilac/plum tiny soil accents. Asymmetrical simple silhouette readable at 110pt wide, transparent background, plenty of transparent margin around compact subject, not a shiny sticker, no outlines, no shadow, no glow, no glitter, no beige backdrop. This is a small narrative detail, not a full landscape.
