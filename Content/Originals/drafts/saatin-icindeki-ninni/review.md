## Kapak sahnesi

Two tiny field mice peek out from the round glass face of an old wall clock inside a small stone station waiting room at dusk: Mısra with sand-coloured fur, a blue knitted beret and a pencil stub in her paws, and Minik with sand-coloured fur and a red knitted beret. Through the frosted window behind them snow falls softly, and far away on the curving rails the Star Train approaches: a small navy-blue steam locomotive with gold stripes, white steam from its chimney, one warm amber headlamp and three little carriages.
