## Analyst summary

Pilot run, re-scoped mid-run at the product owner's request from public-domain classics to contemporary books, because children's books of 1900-1930 differ too much from today's. Five contemporary picture books (Book Dash, CC BY 4.0, South Africa, 2021-2024) were transcribed from page images and analysed page by page with their pictures. Four Internet Archive classics were ingested but not analysed; they stay as a separate, older population. Everything here rests on one publisher in one country: the findings describe this population, not children's books in general. The run also found and fixed a data-quality hole: an IA 'scan' can be a user-made ebook, so uploads are no longer trusted as original page boundaries.
