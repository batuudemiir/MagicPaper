## Blocker and editorial decisions

**Definite character-continuity blocker: pilot56_book_a4_cat_v1 p5.** Exact page: “İlk taşı kendi adım aralığına koydu. Üstüne bastı, taş sallanmadı. Simit çamurlu patisini salladı ve bekledi. Bordo çanta da ayıyla birlikte bekledi.” Simit is the only companion; bear/bag sentence imports another story. Text owner should remove it or use Simit's bell/collar detail. Topic review is complete despite the editorial block.

**Literal depth constraint needs clarification: all A4 books p2.** Mud is presented through shoes sinking; no child immersion is described. Example a4_bear_v1 p2: “Böğürtlen’in kocaman patisi bile batardı.” This hypothetical paw metaphor does not establish actual depth. No current A4 page explicitly bounds mud to shoe level. Add a literal shallow/shoe-level bound if required by authoring contract. Do not infer deep water/quicksand or drowning from the metaphor.

**Rain-paced breathing review: u4 bear/cat/hedgehog event03 p4.** Example u4_bear_event03 p4: “Deniz her damlayı bekledi. Damla düşünce nefes verdi. Damlalar seyreldikçe nefesi de uzadı.” Cat/hedgehog parallels also wait for increasingly sparse drops before exhaling. There is no explicit breath-holding instruction. However readers could interpret waiting as delaying breathing. Suggest normal breathing independent of the drops, while listening/counting remains optional. This is an imitation/timing ambiguity requiring editorial judgement, not a claim that breath-holding is written.

## Safety/agency observations

L4 water is explicitly a shallow garden puddle. Child stays at edge/crouches; no child enters water. Objects placed/retrieved by hand; friend/leaf observations do not imply child immersion. A5 nest scenes observe a distant nest through a separate log hole; no nest entry/touch. A5fox event02 p3 sticks own nose in log hole briefly (“Burnu sıkıştı, sonra pof diye çıktı”): mild comic stuck-nose action, consider simply sniffing outside if imitation avoidance is strict. Nest remains distant. Bear berries come from bag within book, no forest harvesting. No sustained breath-holding written. Lost/mapping decisions remain Deniz's.

- a6_owl_event03 p3: owl already on stone wall with gate “tam önündeydi”, before the child draws map and reaches wall p6–7. Suggest keep owl beside child until arrival.
- a6_hedgehog_v1 p5: “Kestane çizimin yanında bekledi” after they arrived at real rock, while drawing was left on ground at junction. Suggest real rock instead of drawing.
- u4_cat_v1 p5: bell “tın etti” then “artık hiç ses çıkarmıyordu” in same held-bell page. Remove ring or clarify it precedes holding.
- u4_hedgehog_event03 p6 ends waiting (“yenisini beklemedi”), p7 waits for last drop again despite rain already stopped. Retain one last-drop event.
- u6_hedgehog_event02 p4 turns train off; p7 turns train off again. Suggest remove duplicate switch-off.
- l6_hedgehog_event02 p3 falls asleep, p4 follows child walking, p5 “uyanınca”; reorder wake before walking. P7 “sıradaki bankı” introduces another bank for remeasurement; should same bank if unit comparison intended.
- l5_cat_event02 p3 mixes leaves after colour groups; p4 refers to intact two groups. Restore groups or state child is inspecting mixed leaves.
- l4_bear_v1 title/joke “Hep Batar” / “Ben suya girince hep batıyorum” is companion hyperbole, not observed child danger. May confuse swimming facts; consider a weight/size joke rather than a universal sinking claim.
- L4 conclusions “tahta hafif/taş ağır” are local object comparisons; avoid presenting weight alone as a universal flotation rule in supporting educational copy.

## Topic policy

Broad weather includes sunshine/rain/drizzle/wind and explicit cooling. Darkness includes dark/dim scene, night lamp and extinguishing light; greetings/night-blue colour alone excluded. Heights includes hill viewpoint, explicit low-log-disc step/climb and bank slip; ordinary standing/low perch excluded. Alone is not brief nearby camouflage; fear is not surprise. All emotions intensity<=2; none unease/hesitation, so calmEligible reflects current engine rule only.
