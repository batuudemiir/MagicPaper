## Visual storytelling

- The cloud's size tracks the mood: small above his head, then raining, then filling the sky, then a close-up, then fading.
- Bold type marks feeling words on every spread (cheerful, grumpy, sad, joy, smile).

## Strengths

- a feeling made into a visible character whose size shows intensity
- a clear three-try escalation

## Weaknesses

- the child does not solve his own problem
- the last spread states the lesson
