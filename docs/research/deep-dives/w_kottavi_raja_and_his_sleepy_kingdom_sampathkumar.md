## Summary (paraphrase)

A king who dozes all day and lies awake all night collects sleep remedies from his court (a rich dinner, music, a walk, a story) and tries them all in one night, each failing comically; his queen settles him with milk and a massage, a nightly routine slowly works, but his late nights have kept the whole city awake, so the people gather and a spreading wave of yawns brings sleep to everyone.
