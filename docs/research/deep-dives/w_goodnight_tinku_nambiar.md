## Summary (paraphrase)

A pup on a farm cannot fall asleep, slips out into the moonlit night and meets five night animals in turn, each of which agrees to be his friend; tired out by play he goes home, his mother explains that these friends are nocturnal and that he needs sleep, and he sleeps through the night under the smiling moon.

- The moon's face is a silent companion on the first, third and last page.

## Strengths

- a home-away-home bedtime frame: the last page returns to the mother and the same moon at the lowest intensity
- a friendship refrain repeated five times that a listener can join
- the night is reframed from lonely to friendly without any danger

## Weaknesses

- the middle is a list; the five meetings have no rising stakes
- the mother's last speech explains a fact (nocturnal animals) and a rule (you must sleep) in words
