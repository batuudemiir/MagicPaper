## Repetition

- structure: opening and closing walk with the same hand-hold, mood reversed — functions comfort, memory; effective: True — the frame makes the change visible without words
- callback: open books on the floor and thought bubbles recap the dinosaur, fish and rocket — functions memory; effective: True — invites retelling: the child can name each world

## Visual storytelling

- Wordless: every page's information is in the picture, so all page-turn effects are visual.
- Each world dissolves into the next through a shared shape (book edge, starfish into stars, feathers into floor).

## Strengths

- transitions between worlds are built from shapes, so each turn has a small surprise
- clear bookend: the change is shown by the same walk drawn twice

## Weaknesses

- no problem or goal drives the journey; the arc is mood only
- the dinosaur's frown and the shark shadow are left unexplained

## Anti-patterns observed
