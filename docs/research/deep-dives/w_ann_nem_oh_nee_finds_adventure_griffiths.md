## Summary (paraphrase)

A sea anemone stuck to one rock feels lonely and different from every creature that can swim or walk. When a hermit crab is chased by a big fish she shields him with her stings; he wants her courage, she wants his adventures, and by making her laugh off her rock he carries her away on his shell, where she guards him and is never lonely again.
