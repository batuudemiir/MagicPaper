## Summary (paraphrase)

At bedtime a small boy says good night to his family, the sky, the garden and every object in the house; huge shaggy monsters appear in his room, and instead of fleeing he tucks them in, befriends a little one and falls asleep on top of the biggest.

## Strengths

- a bedtime ritual list that calms by rhythm
- the fear peak sits in the middle and the last pages wind down to sleep
- the child tames the fear with the ritual he already owns; no adult intervenes

## Weaknesses
