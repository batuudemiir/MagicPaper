## Summary (paraphrase)

Khuzwayo loves to dance and is chosen to perform the Ingoma harvest dance. Afraid of dancing before people from many villages, he says he cannot do it and runs off. The village searches; his two best friends find him in the maize field. He tells them he is nervous of making a mistake, and they practise with him until he feels ready. On the day he leads his village to the arena, a little nervous still, and his dance is loved by all.

## Strengths

- fear is named in the child's own words and answered by peers, not by an adult
- a wordless reveal spread at the centre of the book
- the fear is reduced, not erased: still a little nervous on the day
- picture scale tracks how big or small he feels
- a culturally specific occasion explained in one sentence

## Weaknesses

- the solution is told in a line (grows more confident) rather than shown through an attempt or setback
- the dance itself is over in one spread; the climax is brief
- the two friends have no names or voices of their own beyond helping
- longest sentence 18 words on the procession page

## Anti-patterns observed

- resolves_too_quickly (pages [9, 10]): one practice session and a statement of growing confidence carry him from fleeing to ready; no failed attempt
