## Analyst interpretation (one book; not a finding)

- OBSERVED: a circular night frame. Spreads 1 and 12 use the same bedroom layout, and the change is shown by one detail in the window: the tap stops dripping and the curtain is tied back. The book ends on sleep. It is a natural bedtime-mode shape: the problem is raised at night and settled by the next night.
- OBSERVED: an adult does the fix (the grandmother calls a repairer, who mends the tap). The child's part is noticing, reporting and one failed attempt, and he is credited afterwards with a thumbs-up and a shared hand on the tap.
