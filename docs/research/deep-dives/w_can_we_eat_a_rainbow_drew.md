## Summary (paraphrase)

Pabi and her dog Boo set out to find a rainbow with Boo's nose. For each of four colours the nose first finds something that cannot be eaten (socks, soap, a bottle, a ball) and then foods of that colour that can. The finds are gathered, cooked into a colourful meal, and eaten; the last line tells the listener that they can eat a rainbow too.

## Repetition

- refrain: Sniff, sniff, sniff. on every wrong-find spread — functions participation, rhythm; effective: True — a short sound line the listener can join, set in bold, always in the same slot
- structure: wrong find, Can we eat ...? No!, unfinished line; then food word, Can we eat ...? Yes!, adjectives, question to the listener — functions participation, anticipation, rhythm; effective: True — the yes/no answer is given by the listener before the text gives it; four rounds without variation in shape risk monotony but each round changes colour and joke
- predictable_pattern: Can you think of something <colour> that is good to eat? closes each food spread — functions participation; effective: True — hands the turn to the listener at a fixed point; asks for the listener's own answer, not one in the book
- cumulative: the four finds are listed again with their colours — functions memory; effective: True — recall before the idea; prepares the rainbow meal

## Strengths

- a question the listener can shout the answer to on every spread
- unfinished line at each wrong-find spread end, completed by a food after the turn
- a colour concept carried by a quest instead of a list
- title question asked and answered at the end
- the listener is invited to name their own food on four spreads

## Weaknesses

- no problem or stakes: the hunt never fails
- four identical rounds; the shape never varies
- the rainbow is four colours in the text and six in the picture
- the adult who cooks is invisible in the text, so the we-cooked claim belongs to an unseen helper
- a health message is stated at the end (makes us big and strong)
- Boo is referred to with her once and his/its elsewhere only by name

## Anti-patterns observed

- moral_stated_not_shown (pages [12]): the meal makes us big and strong is told, not shown; mild, and typical of concept books
- boring_repetition (pages [2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9]): eight spreads in one fixed two-step shape; the jokes and colours vary but the structure never does. Borderline: for 3-4 the predictability may be the point
