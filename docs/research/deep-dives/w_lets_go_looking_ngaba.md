## Summary (paraphrase)

At school playtime a group of children go looking for anything at all: one finds a nest, one a snail, one smells rain, one feels the wind, and one sees a dragon hiding in the clouds. The bell calls them inside, the finds are listed again, and the reader is asked what they found.

## Interpretation (analyst, one book)

This is one book; everything below is a hypothesis.

- OBSERVED: the strongest page turn is a single sentence split across spreads 9 and 10, with the picture showing the hiding place before the words finish. It works read aloud even without pictures.
- OBSERVED: a school bell opens and closes the book, and the last line hands the question to the listener. A ritual frame plus a question to the reader is the book's closing design.
- OBSERVED: Emma's find (rain) is missing from the final recap, so a cumulative recap can drop an item without anyone in the story noticing; a generator that recaps should check that every item comes back.
- OBSERVED: no adult appears and there is no problem to solve; the book is discovery without stakes.
